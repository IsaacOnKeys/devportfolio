# Portfolio Website — Purpose

This repository hosts my professional portfolio website. Its sole purpose is to clearly show what I work on and why it matters—fast.

## What This Site Delivers
- **Clarity for hiring teams:** Quick, honest summaries of projects and outcomes.
- **Business impact first:** What problems were solved and what decisions the work enables.
- **Signal over noise:** Minimal jargon; no implementation deep dives.
- **Easy contact:** A simple path to reach me for roles or collaboration.

## Who It’s For
Recruiters, hiring managers, and collaborators who want a concise view of my capabilities and results.

## What You’ll Find
- **Project Overviews:** Problem → approach (plain language) → outcome.
- **Results & Visuals:** Key findings and takeaways at a glance.
- **About Me & Contact:** Context on my focus areas and how to get in touch.

## Status
Actively maintained; updated as new work ships.
